Privacy

What the game keeps about you, and why. It’s short, because we keep little.

Playing as a guest

You don’t need an account to play. As a guest your game is saved in this browser. Unless you turn it off under Sync devices, a copy of that save is also kept on our save service after a minute of play, under a short sync code, so you can carry on from another device. No name or email address goes with it.

What an account keeps

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • Your saves: up to ten save slots, each with a short summary (where you are, time played, how much is complete).
  • Your play statistics: time played, the milestones you reach and when, boss fights, deaths, completion, badges and rank. They make up your Keeper’s Record and your times on the Keepers’ Board.
  • A keeper name, if you choose one, and a crew code if you join a crew.
  • The devices you’re signed in on: a short label such as “Firefox on Windows” and when each was last used, so you can sign out everywhere.
  • A password, only if you set one, and only in a scrambled one-way form that can’t be read back.

Why

Your saves are kept so you can carry on from any device. Your play statistics fill your Record and the board, and show us where players get stuck so we can make the game better. Your keeper name is how you appear on the board; without one you aren’t listed.

Your email address

It is used only to sign you in (an emailed link and a six-digit code) and for messages about your account: when your password changes, and when your account is deleted. It is never shown to other players. The emails are sent through an email delivery service (Resend).

Anonymous usage pings

While you play, the game sends a few anonymous pings at points along the way (starting, beating a boss, a death and so on), with the game’s version and whether you play with a keyboard, a controller or touch. They carry no name, no email address and no account, only a random number that is forgotten when you close the game. If your browser asks sites not to track it (Do Not Track), none are sent.

On this device

The game keeps your save, your settings and any sync code in this browser’s storage. Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback, we get what you write, the game’s version, how you play (keyboard, controller or touch) and your current objective, plus a way to reach you only if you add one.

Deleting your account

In the game, open Account, choose Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the game asks you to sign in again first. This deletes your account for good: your email address, your save slots, your Record and board times, your keeper name and your signed-in devices. One last email confirms it. Feedback you’ve sent and the anonymous pings aren’t tied to your account, so they stay as they are. You can carry on playing as a guest.

Who can have an account

Accounts are for people 13 and over.

A game in beta

Brave Quest: Lighthouse is still in beta, so things change. If what we keep changes, this page changes with it.

Get in touch

A question, or something here that looks wrong? Use Send feedback in the game (on the title screen and in the pause menu), and add your email address if you’d like a reply.
